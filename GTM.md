# GTM

## Target User
Primary target: Head of Engineering or CTO at a 10-80 person B2B SaaS company spending $200–$2,000/month on AI tools. They already pay for 2–5 AI products (Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, API usage) and care about ROI, not novelty. Secondary target: technical founders running a lean team who want to cut unnecessary recurring spend.

## Trigger Moments
- Finance asks engineering to cut SaaS spend this quarter.
- The team adds a third AI tool and the monthly bill spikes.
- A founder reads a thread about “AI tool sprawl” and wants a benchmark.
- A head of engineering does a quarterly vendor review or a budget reforecast.

## Distribution Channels
- Subreddits: r/SaaS, r/startups, r/EntrepreneurRideAlong.
- Communities: Indie Hackers, YC Hacker News (Show HN), Founder Slack groups.
- X (Twitter): lists of startup founders and CTOs discussing AI stack decisions.
- Newsletters: “B2B SaaS Weekly,” engineering leader digests.

## First 100 Users in 30 Days (No Paid Budget)
Week 1: Post a Show HN with a crisp pitch and a visible demo. Target 500 visitors, 120 audits. Week 2: Write one tactical blog post (“AI tool spend audit: how to cut $500/mo in 20 minutes”) and distribute in 3 founder communities. Week 3: DM 30 founders who publicly discuss AI costs with a personalized audit offer. Week 4: Add a lightweight “audit your stack” widget to 3 partner blogs or newsletters. Goal: 100 completed audits and 20 qualified leads.

## Unfair Advantage
Credex has access to discounted AI credits and aggregate market pricing knowledge. The audit can recommend direct savings paths that competitors cannot offer (specific “buy credits instead of retail” paths).
