# METRICS

## North Star Metric
Qualified consultations booked per week. This reflects the core business goal (Credex revenue) and captures whether the audit drives real demand.

## Input Metrics
1. Audit completion rate (visits -> completed audits)
2. Lead capture rate (completed audits -> email captured)
3. Consultation booking rate (email captured -> consultation booked)

## First Instrumentation
- Track audit starts and completions
- Track lead form submits and failures
- Track consultation CTA clicks
- Track summary generation success/fallback rate

## Pivot Trigger
If audit completion stays below 10% after 2 weeks of 1,000+ visitors, the form UX or positioning needs a redesign.
