# ECONOMICS

Credex earns revenue when a lead converts to a credit purchase. I assume an average purchase of $20k in credits with a 15% gross margin ($3k gross profit). If 40% of qualified consultations close, then the expected value per qualified consultation is $1.2k (0.4 * $3k). If 20% of completed audits become qualified consultations, the expected value per completed audit is $240.

## Lead Value Assumptions
- Average credit purchase: $20,000
- Gross margin: 15% ($3,000)
- Consultation to purchase: 40%
- Expected value per consultation: $1,200
- Audit to qualified consultation: 20%
- Expected value per completed audit: $240

## CAC by Channel
- Founder communities (Indie Hackers, Slack groups): ~$5–$15 per completed audit (time cost)
- Show HN: ~$2–$8 per completed audit (one-time post, large reach)
- Founder DMs: ~$10–$25 per completed audit (manual effort)

## Funnel Conversion Targets
- Visit -> Audit completion: 20%
- Audit completion -> Email capture: 20%
- Email capture -> Consultation booked: 15–25%
- Consultation -> Purchase: 30–50%

## $1M ARR in 18 Months Math
Target ARR $1,000,000 with 15% margin means $6.67M in annual credit purchases.
If average purchase is $20k, that is ~334 purchases per year (6.67M / 20k).
At 40% close rate from consultations, we need 835 consultations annually.
At 20% audit-to-consultation, we need 4,175 completed audits annually.
That is ~350 audits/month or ~12 audits/day. This is achievable with consistent distribution and a product that converts at the above benchmarks.
