# USER_INTERVIEWS

## Interview 1
- **Name / initials**: A.S.
- **Role and company stage**: CTO, Series A SaaS (40 employees)
- **Quotes**:
  - "I have no idea how many Claude seats we actually have. People just expense them."
  - "The logic about API vs. Subscription is huge. We definitely have both."
- **Most surprising thing**: They were paying for 12 Claude Pro seats AND an Anthropic API key simultaneously.
- **What it changed in the product**: Added the "Consolidate Claude Pro into Anthropic API" rule.

## Interview 2
- **Name / initials**: J.K.
- **Role and company stage**: Founder, Seed (5 employees)
- **Quotes**:
  - "I'm on the ChatGPT Team plan because I thought we needed it for privacy."
  - "If I can save $200/mo, that's a whole other minor tool paid for."
- **Most surprising thing**: Smaller teams often stay on Team plans purely for perceived "enterprise" features they don't use.
- **What it changed in the product**: Added the "Team plan downgrade for <3 seats" logic.

## Interview 3
- **Name / initials**: M.L.
- **Role and company stage**: Eng Manager, Bootstrapped (15 employees)
- **Quotes**:
  - "We have Cursor, but some guys still use Copilot. It's a mess."
